Your Night Creates More

More than a venue. A nonprofit built for community impact.

Every show helps the Santa Barbara Bowl Foundation care for this historic venue, expand access to music and advance a more sustainable live-event experience.

When you attend a show, donate or support the Bowl, you help turn unforgettable live music into lasting cultural, educational and environmental benefit.
Greener Shows Bike valet, reusable cups, waste sorting and responsible venue operations.
Music Access Programs that connect young people and community members with live music.
Future Artists Instruments, grants, scholarships and opportunities for the next generation.
A Venue Worth Protecting Stewardship of a treasured historic amphitheater and its surrounding land.
