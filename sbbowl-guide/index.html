Please Plan Accordingly

  • Accessible parking: Park in designated accessible locations at Santa Barbara High School.
  • Drop-off: Use the corner of Milpas and Anapamu.
  • Rideshare: Arrange drop-off at Milpas and Anapamu.

Accessible Trolleys

Accessible trolleys run throughout the night between the Lower Plaza and the Main Plaza. There is no trolley transportation to or from Santa Barbara High School or the Armory.

If a guest arrives needing mobility assistance, please speak with event staff for trolley accommodations.

Hearing Loop

The seating area is looped for high-quality assistive listening.

Coming & Going

Please arrive early!
The Santa Barbara Bowl has a single point of entry, and entry lines can move slowly—especially close to showtime.

Bike Valet (Free!)
Ride your bike and use the free Bike Valet provided by Move Santa Barbara, conveniently located near the main entrance.

Drop-Offs
Taxi, Uber, Lyft and personal vehicle drop-offs must use the zone on Milpas Street in front of the Bowl. Travel northbound on Milpas to access it.

Pick-Ups After the Show
Once streets close, use the Santa Barbara High School entrance on Anapamu Street. The cab line is on Milpas at Figueroa.

Parking
Public parking is available for $30 at:

  • Santa Barbara High School — enter on Anapamu Street
  • The Armory — enter on Nopal Street

Security

  • All patrons are subject to a security check upon entrance.
  • Please keep cell phone use to a minimum.
  • Do not bring large bags or purses.
  • Small handheld bags, purses or clutches may be no larger than 10″ × 7″ × 2″. Infant and medical bags may be allowed after review by security.

Wristbands

Wristbands are required for:

  • Sections P, R, S, T, U and V — Floor
  • W1 and W2 — Accessible Seating
  • Alcohol purchases for guests 21 and older

Multiple wristband locations are available, and each can provide every wristband you need. Event staff may verify credentials and enforce access restrictions at any time.
